🌱 GreenPulse — Tree Planting Verification

Plant a tree. Get certified. Make an impact that lasts.

GreenPulse lets you verify your tree planting with a tamper-proof photo and GPS location — and instantly receive an official PDF certificate confirming your contribution to a cleaner planet.

HOW IT WORKS:
1. Open the app and tap "Verify Planting"
2. Take a live photo of your seedling with the built-in camera
3. Our AI identifies the tree species (powered by AWS Rekognition)
4. Receive your official certificate with CO₂ absorption calculated by IPCC Tier 1 methodology
5. Share your impact with the world

WHY GREENPULSE IS DIFFERENT:
✅ Anti-fraud technology — only live camera photos accepted, gallery blocked
✅ GPS-verified location embedded in every certificate
✅ AI species recognition with 90%+ accuracy
✅ CO₂ calculation based on IPCC Tier 1 scientific methodology
✅ Scannable QR code for public certificate verification
✅ Fully GDPR-compliant — your data is yours

WHAT YOUR CERTIFICATE INCLUDES:
• Tree species (Latin and common name)
• Verified GPS coordinates (privacy-rounded to 10m)
• Exact date and time of planting
• Annual CO₂ absorption estimate (kg/year)
• Unique QR code for public verification
• Your name as the verified planter

PRIVACY & SECURITY:
• GPS coordinates rounded to protect your exact location
• Photos stored with end-to-end encryption (EU servers)
• Delete your account and all data anytime (GDPR Article 17)
• No selling of personal data — ever

Join thousands of people making verified environmental impact.

One tree. One certificate. One step toward a greener planet.
